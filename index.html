About

Photography that feels like a memory.

I'm Manyeyo — a lifestyle photographer based in Accra and Akosombo, Ghana, and available to travel. Over the past two years, I've been building a practice around a simple idea: the best photographs don't look posed. They look remembered.

My work lives at the intersection of editorial polish and genuine human warmth — the kind of images that families frame and brands build campaigns around. I also love making memories through collages, bringing meaningful moments together in one creative keepsake. Beyond photography, I have an interest in modelling and exploring creative expression in front of the camera as well as behind it.
