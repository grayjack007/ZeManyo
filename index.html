About

Photography that feels like a memory.

I'm Manyeyo — a lifestyle photographer based in Accra and available wherever the light is good. For over a decade I've built a practice around a simple idea: the best photographs don't look posed. They look remembered.

My work lives at the intersection of editorial polish and genuine human warmth — the kind of images that families frame and brands build campaigns around.
